VIDEO LOCALIZATION FOR CREATORS

Bring your videos to Vietnamese audiences.

VietDub Studio is a Windows desktop tool that helps creators transcribe, translate, dub, preview, and publish videos they own or are authorized to use.

How it works

  1. The creator selects an owned MP4 or authorized source video.
  2. The app produces a Vietnamese voice track and Vietnamese subtitles.
  3. The creator reviews the completed video and chooses whether to download it or post it.
  4. Posting starts only after the creator connects TikTok, selects all post settings, and expressly confirms the upload.

The current release is a local educational preview. TikTok posting is limited to authorized Sandbox target users and private visibility until TikTok approves a production integration.
