Privacy Policy

This policy explains what VietDub Studio processes when you create a localized video or connect a TikTok account.

Data processed on your computer

The app processes source videos, extracted audio, transcripts, translations, generated speech, subtitles, completed videos, job status, and local diagnostic information. By default, the AI pipeline runs on the user's Windows computer.

TikTok information

After you authorize TikTok, the app receives an access token, refresh token, granted scopes, and basic creator information such as username and nickname. Tokens are encrypted before being stored on the same computer. They are used only to retrieve current posting options, upload a video you expressly approve, and check its publishing status.

Third-party services

When TikTok posting is used, approved video and post settings are sent to TikTok. If the optional OpenAI provider is enabled by the user, relevant audio or text is sent to OpenAI for the selected AI operations. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing and sale

VietDub Studio has no central analytics or advertising service and does not sell personal information. Data is shared only when the user enables and invokes a required third-party feature.

Retention and deletion

Project files, completed media, job records, and encrypted TikTok credentials remain on the user's computer until the user deletes them. TikTok access can also be revoked from the connected TikTok account.

Security

The app binds its local server to the loopback interface, uses OAuth state and PKCE, and encrypts stored TikTok tokens. No system can eliminate every risk, so users should protect their Windows account and never share application secrets.

Policy updates

This page will be updated when data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
